Rules & Regulations

Please read all rules carefully before participating. Compliance ensures a fair and professional environment for all elite performers.

1.

The competition is open to all participants (school/college not mandatory).

2.

Each participant must complete registration before the deadline.

3.

Any dance style is allowed (open style).

4.

Participants must carry their own music in MP3 format (with backup).

5.

Props are allowed but must be safe and self-managed.

6.

Participants must report at least 30–60 minutes before the event.

7.

No vulgar, offensive, or inappropriate songs/movements are allowed.

8.

Use of dangerous items (fire, water, sharp objects, etc.) is strictly prohibited.

9.

Participants must maintain discipline and proper stage conduct.

10.

The judges’ decision will be final and binding.

Performance Standards

At DDC, we uphold the highest standards of professional artistry. Every participant is expected to bring their absolute best to the stage while respecting the craft and their fellow competitors.
